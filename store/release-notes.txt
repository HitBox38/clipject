ClipJect 1.0.1 — more reliable snippet insertion.

- Keep Enter and arrow keys working normally while typing in webpage fields.
- Undo and redo snippet insertion with the browser's native editing history.
- Keep snippets for repeated fields separate, even when fields share a name.
- Use the snippet picker inside native webpage dialogs.
- Prevent insertion into fields that become read-only, disabled, or unsupported.
- Reject snippets that exceed a field's maximum length without changing its value.

Saved snippets stay on your device. This update adds no permissions or data collection.
